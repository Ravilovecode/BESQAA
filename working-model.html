How BESQAA Works

The BESQAA Purchase Assistance Model

From saving your Buy-Back Date to delivery on the day you become eligible to purchase

👇 Click on any step below for detailed information

1
📅

Enter Your Buy-Back Date

Voluntary — only the date is required

A PSU officer or employee can voluntarily enter their fixed Buy-Back Date in the BESQAA app or on our website. The Buy-Back Amount may also be entered, but this is optional.

Only Buy-Back Date is required. Buy-Back Amount is optional. No personal information will be requested.

2
🔔

Reminder About 15 Days in Advance

No need to keep checking your Furniture Card

BESQAA uses your saved date to plan a reminder approximately 15 days before your Buy-Back Date, so you don't have to track the date manually.

3
🔍

Research, Compare & Choose

A 15-day product-research window, without being rushed

After the reminder, explore the available product range and compare brands, models, features and prices — online and offline. Then decide whether you'd like to place an order. There is no obligation.

4
🤝

Order Confirmation in Advance

Product, model, price, address and delivery schedule — all confirmed with you

If you choose to proceed, BESQAA confirms the selected product, model, price, delivery address and delivery schedule with you before the order is processed. You'll also see product availability, delivery terms, warranty and return/replacement conditions before you confirm.

If you've found a lower price elsewhere, share the comparable listing or quotation at this stage so it can be checked under our Minimum Market Price Guarantee.

5
🚚

Zero-Day Delivery on Your Buy-Back Date

The first day you are eligible to purchase

BESQAA arranges your confirmed product and aims to deliver it on your Buy-Back Date itself. Delivery on the Buy-Back Date is subject to product availability, location, order confirmation and applicable operational conditions.

Price Commitment — Minimum Market Price Guarantee

You are free to research prices online and offline and choose the product that suits you. For the same product, model and comparable terms of sale, BESQAA commits to offering a price at least ₹1 lower than the comparable market price presented and verified at the time of order confirmation.

How comparable prices are assessed:

  • Share the comparable listing or quotation so the comparison can be checked.
  • The comparison takes account of the seller, product configuration and model.
  • Warranty, delivery charges, availability and offer conditions are also considered.
  • The price is verified at the time of order confirmation.

Important: Purchase Eligibility

The purchase must be made on or after your applicable Buy-Back Date and remains subject to your PSU's rules and eligibility conditions. Purchase eligibility, reimbursement and allowance rules vary by organisation and may be subject to internal policies. Please confirm the purchase rules that apply to you with your organisation.

The Buy-Back Date feature is only for PSU customers who choose to use it. Members of the general public can browse and order normally through the website or mobile app.
