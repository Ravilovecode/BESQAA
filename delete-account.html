Delete Your Account

Besqaa mobile app — BESQAA.in

You can delete your Besqaa app account, and the personal data linked to it, at any time. Choose either option below.

Option 1: Delete in the Besqaa app (instant)

  1. Open the Besqaa app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Delete account.
  3. Enter your password and tap Delete my account, then confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: Request deletion without the app

Email us at besqaa4india@gmail.com from the email address registered with your account, with the subject "Delete my Besqaa account". You can also call us on +91-7368919823.

We will verify that the request comes from the account owner and delete your account within 7 working days, then confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, mobile number and GST number
  • Saved delivery addresses
  • Saved products and your cart
  • Buyback details
  • Your profile photo
  • Product queries you sent

What we keep, and for how long

Records of orders you placed (items, amounts, delivery address and any payment screenshot) are kept for the period required by Indian tax and accounting law, and are then deleted. After your account is deleted, these records are no longer linked to your account.

For more details, see our Privacy Policy.
