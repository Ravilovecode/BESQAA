About BESQAA.in

A customer-first shopping experience for PSU officers, employees and the general public

BESQAA.in — E-Commerce Platform

BESQAA.in is an e-commerce platform that enables customers to shop online through its website and dedicated mobile application. The platform brings together a wide range of products across consumer electronics, home appliances, kitchen appliances, furniture, mobile phones, laptops, printers and other everyday product categories.

BESQAA is primarily designed to serve officers and employees of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), while also welcoming members of the general public to browse products and place orders through the platform.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make product purchasing simpler, more convenient and more economical for PSU officers and employees by aligning shopping support, timely reminders and delivery planning with their applicable purchase or Buy-Back dates.

We also aim to offer the wider public a convenient online shopping destination with a broad product range and customer-focused service.

Our Commitment to Customers

At BESQAA, customers should be able to choose products with confidence, compare available options and receive clear information before placing an order. For PSU customers who voluntarily register their Buy-Back Date, BESQAA aims to provide timely reminders, a useful product-research window and delivery coordinated with the first eligible purchase date.

Why Choose BESQAA?

  • Zero-Day Delivery — we aim to deliver your confirmed product on your Buy-Back Date
  • Minimum Market Price Guarantee — at least ₹1 lower than the verified comparable market price
  • Timely reminders about 15 days before your Buy-Back Date
  • Only Buy-Back Date required — Buy-Back Amount optional, no personal information requested
