About BESQAA.in

BESQAA.in is an e-commerce platform that lets customers shop online through its website and dedicated mobile application. It brings together a wide range of products across consumer electronics, home appliances, kitchen appliances, furniture, mobile phones, laptops, printers and other everyday categories.

🏢

Who We Serve

BESQAA is primarily designed for officers and employees of Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and also welcomes members of the general public to browse products and place orders.

🎯

Our Mission

To make product purchasing simpler, more convenient and more economical for PSU officers and employees by aligning shopping support, timely reminders and delivery planning with their applicable purchase or Buy-Back dates, and to offer the wider public a convenient online shopping destination with customer-focused service.

🤝

Our Commitment

Choose products with confidence, compare available options and get clear information before placing an order. For PSU customers who voluntarily register their Buy-Back Date, we aim to provide timely reminders, a useful product-research window and delivery coordinated with the first eligible purchase date.

Three Major Benefits for PSU Officers & Employees

🚚

Zero-Day Delivery

We aim to deliver your confirmed product on the Buy-Back Date itself — the first day on which you are eligible to make the purchase. Contacting you around 15 days in advance gives time to confirm the product and prepare for delivery.

Delivery on the Buy-Back Date is subject to product availability, location, order confirmation and applicable operational conditions.

₹

Minimum Market Price Guarantee

Research prices online and offline and choose what suits you. For the same product, model and comparable terms of sale, BESQAA commits to a price at least ₹1 lower than the comparable market price presented and verified at the time of order confirmation.

Share the comparable listing or quotation so it can be checked. How prices are compared →

🔔

Timely Reminders & a 15-Day Research Window

Save your Buy-Back Date and we aim to remind you about 15 days before it — no need to keep checking your Furniture Card or tracking the date yourself. Use that time to research products and compare brands and prices without being rushed.

How It Works

  1. 1

    Enter your Buy-Back Date

    Voluntarily save your fixed Buy-Back Date. The Buy-Back Amount is optional.

  2. 2

    Get a reminder

    We aim to remind you about 15 days before your Buy-Back Date.

  3. 3

    Research & choose

    Explore our range and compare brands, models, features and prices.

  4. 4

    Confirm your order

    We confirm the product, model, price, delivery address and schedule with you in advance.

  5. 5

    Delivery on your Buy-Back Date

    We coordinate delivery for your Buy-Back Date, the first day you are eligible to purchase.
